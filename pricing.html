Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really a free tier?

Yes — and it's not a trial. Every tool is free and open source under the MIT license. Install from GitHub, use all CLI features, no account needed. The free tier has no rate limits for local development.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you all CLI tools with full features. Pro adds hosted CI/CD services (automated PR gating, Slack alerts, team dashboards), unlimited usage for high-volume teams, and priority support. The CLI tools themselves work the same way in both tiers.

How does licensing work?

For the free tier, no license key is needed — just install and run. When Pro launches, you'll set a DEVFORGE_LICENSE_KEY environment variable (JWT-based) to unlock hosted services. No account or login required.

When will Pro launch?

We're building the hosted services now. Join the waitlist above and we'll notify you. The free CLI tools are available today and will remain free forever.

Can I use these tools commercially?

Yes. The MIT license allows commercial use. You can use all CLI tools in your company's CI/CD pipelines, on your servers, and in your products. The only restriction is that you can't resell the tools themselves as your own product.

Do you offer student or open-source discounts?

The free tier covers all open-source and student use cases. When Pro launches, active open-source maintainers will get free Pro access — email us at oss@devforge.
